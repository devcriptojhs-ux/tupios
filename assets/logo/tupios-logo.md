# TUPIOS Logo

## Description
Centrally aligned logo design against a black background. At the top center is a diamond-shaped emblem made of four distinct quadrants around a black four-pointed star center. Beneath the emblem, centered horizontally, is the word "TUPIOS" written in a wide, modern, sans-serif font.

## Colors
- **Background**: Solid black (#000000)
- **Emblem**: White (#FFFFFF) for top-left, bottom-left, and bottom-right quadrants; bright neon green (#52FF12 / #4EFF00) for top-right quadrant
- **Text**: "TUPI" in white (#FFFFFF); "OS" in bright neon green (#52FF12 / #4EFF00)

## Elements
- Central logo icon: rounded diamond outline divided into 4 corner quadrants, forming a black negative-space four-pointed star in the middle
  - Top-left quadrant: White geometric pattern
  - Top-right quadrant: Neon green L-shaped angular fill
  - Bottom-left quadrant: White angular geometric lines
  - Bottom-right quadrant: White geometric pattern resembling abstract stylized lines
- Text element: "TUPIOS" in uppercase sans-serif typography with rounded outer corners and square inner lines

## Usage
- Boot splash ASCII art (see `/etc/tupi/boot-logo.txt`)
- Desktop wallpaper / login screen
- Documentation headers
- ISO installer branding
